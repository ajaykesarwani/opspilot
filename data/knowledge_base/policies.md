# Operations Policy

## Incident Response
When a P1 incident occurs, the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 5 minutes. If not acknowledged, it escalates to the secondary.
A P1 incident requires an immediate war room and a post-mortem within 48 hours of resolution.

## Billing Disputes
Refunds under $50 can be approved directly by Tier 1 support.
Refunds between $50 and $500 require Tier 2 approval.
Refunds over $500 require manager approval.

## Account Deletion
Account deletion is a hard delete and takes 30 days to process completely to comply with GDPR. Data cannot be recovered after this period.

## Access Requests
VPN access requests must go through the IT portal and require manager approval. Contractors are only granted VPN access for the duration of their contract.
